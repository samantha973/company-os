Bunnings launch — media targeting plan

The verticals we're targeting for the TAXIBOX × Bunnings announcement, in the order we'll work them, with example titles, the lead headline, and the angle for each stream.

Core streams

01

Tier 1 — National business press

Lead
Australian Financial ReviewThe Australian (Business)Capital BriefSky News Business / ausbiz

Strongest headline

It now costs more than $80,000 to move house — so Bunnings is moving into storage instead

The macro-pegged flagship. As moving costs climb into six figures for a capital-city upgrade, Australians renovate rather than relocate — a ~$44bn renovation market (HIA), growing about twice as fast as the economy. Bunnings, the home of the renovation, is monetising the storage-and-moving squeeze that creates, via challenger TAXIBOX, reaching ~80% of Australians. The story is a retail giant's move into the economics of staying put, and the established, national challenger it backed (a brand-awareness gap of ~18% vs ~50% for incumbents — source to be confirmed with client). Interviews: Bunnings COO + TAXIBOX CEO.

02

Property & commercial real estate

Light now · big story held
The Urban DeveloperAustralian Property JournalrealestatesourceAFR Property

Strongest headline

Denser homes, pricier moves: Bunnings and TAXIBOX target the storage gap in Australian housing

Homes are shrinking and moves are costlier, fuelling demand for flexible storage (a $2.5bn industry, IBISWorld). Bunnings' entry is a read on where housing is heading. Note: the stronger property story — TAXIBOX Local's national site rollout (50–200 sites, freehold/leasehold) — is held as a separate exclusive for the property desk (Sarah Petty, AFR). This vertical gets a light touch now, the main play later.

03

Mid-tier business — SME & founder

Wave 2
SmartCompanyDynamic BusinessStartup DailyKochie's Business Builders

Strongest headline

Why Bunnings chose TAXIBOX: how a proven challenger brand earned a partnership with a giant

A founder story about strength, not luck. When Australia's most trusted retailer decided to move into moving and storage, it didn't build its own — it chose TAXIBOX. What made it the obvious partner: an already-national operator with 15 years and hundreds of thousands of moves behind it (250k+ customers, 10k+ units), a customer experience that outrates the traditional industry, a flexible door-to-door model, and the tech to plug straight into Bunnings' national network. The lesson for founders — build enough credibility, scale and reliability and the biggest players come to you. Founder interview with Ben Cohn.

04

National consumer — news & broadcast

Wave 3
news.com.auThe Daily TelegraphHerald SunA Current Affair / Today / Sunrise

Strongest headline

Where do you put your house while you rebuild it? Now it's one trip — to Bunnings

The relatable human yarn. Moving is expensive, so Australians renovate — and every reno creates the "where does everything go?" headache. The new Bunnings service turns the storage-shed trek into a single Bunnings trip (get a sausage and sort your storage). Told through a real family mid-reno — bespoke, filmable talent we can offer one outlet exclusively. Strong TV and tabloid material.

New on-site storage option for builders as Bunnings adds moving and storage

For builders and construction readers: a practical new way to get storage to the job site — a container delivered to site or yard, used as an extension of the shed, then moved or stored. Personal finance & money

Addition Wave 2

Strongest headline

It costs $80,000+ to move house — the maths driving Australians to renovate, and store, instead

A money-desk explainer riding the cost-of-living peg. Stamp duty and moving costs now run into six figures, making "improve, don't move" the rational choice — and storage is the hidden line item. Data-led, with cost breakdowns and practical tips, plus the new lower-friction Bunnings/TAXIBOX option as the "smart money" angle.

The renovation headache every listener knows — and the one-trip fix at Bunnings

Radio is the fastest relatable reach for this story. Talkback loves a "have you ever" hook — the storage-shed trek, the reno chaos — and Ben (or the case-study family) makes an easy, warm segment. Metro talkback for reach; ABC Mornings for the cost-of-living framing. Low lift, high relatability.

Sequencing

Lead: Tier 1 business (AFR), interview-led, sets the agenda. Wave 2 (on publication): SME/founder, retail trade, personal finance. Wave 3 (with the case study): consumer news & broadcast, home/reno lifestyle, radio. Ongoing: building and logistics/removals/storage trade. Held: TAXIBOX Local's national rollout — the bigger property story, pitched separately once Bunnings has run.
