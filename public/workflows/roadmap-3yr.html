Three shifts that get you there

A$8M at 45% net with you out of the day-to-day isn't one big leap — it's three deliberate shifts, made in order.

SHIFT 01

You: doer → leader → face

Delivering the work → leading the people → being the brand & the strategy. Each year you hand one layer down.

SHIFT 02

Labour → leverage

People doing everything → senior people directing an AI bench. This is what makes 45% net possible where rivals sit at 15%.

SHIFT 03

Practice → asset

A firm that needs you → a firm that owns its IP, its clients & its rhythm. Founder-independent, and therefore valuable.

What has to be true for 45% net

45% net profit is the whole game — and it's rare. Traditional agencies never get near it because payroll eats 55–60% of revenue. Your one structural advantage makes it possible: keep payroll under ~45% of revenue by letting AI carry the junior load.

The typical agency

Payroll (pyramid of juniors)55–60%
Overheads & delivery20–25%
Net profit10–18%

The PR Hub at Year 3

Net profit45%
The four levers that hold 45%

1 · Payroll ratio ≤ 45%. Every senior lead must carry enough revenue (via AI leverage) to keep this ratio. Watch it like a hawk.

2 · Price on value, raise every year. Premium tiers climb A$11k → A$13–15k as authority compounds. Higher price, same cost = pure margin.

3 · The AI bench is the margin. It's what lets 8 leads do the work of an agency of 40. Protect and reinvest in it as your #1 asset.

4 · No idle seniors, no scope creep. Utilisation and disciplined scope are where services-firm margin quietly dies.
