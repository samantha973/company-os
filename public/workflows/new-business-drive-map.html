Every prospect folder is built the same way. A prospect stays here until they sign — then their live work moves into the client Drive system and this remains the new-business record.

How a prospect flows through

  • 1Prospect is created under the [MONTH] they’re first talked to, then their own [PROSPECT NAME] folder.
  • 2The initial call/meeting transcript is filed in INITIAL DISCOVERY.
  • 3The deck is built and presented from PRESENTATION DECK › PRESENTATION LIVE.
  • 4After live feedback, the tweaked, confirmed deck goes in PRESENTATION SENT — workable file + PDF — and that’s what’s sent over.
  • 5If they sign, the work moves into the client Drive system; this stays as the new-business record.

Two deck folders — why

  • →PRESENTATION LIVE — the version we present in the room and gather feedback on.
  • →PRESENTATION SENT — the same deck after any post-meeting tweaks, ready to send. Keeping both preserves what was shown vs. what was sent.
  • →Each holds a workable file (Word/HTML) so it can be edited, plus a PDF for sending.
