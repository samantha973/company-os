New Business Filing

Where potential-client (new business) activity is filed — kept entirely separate from live client folders. This whole area is internal: the prospect never sees it.

🔒 Separate to CLIENTS. A prospect is filed here until they sign. When they become a client, their live work moves into the client Drive system — this stays as the new-business record.

The process

The meeting lead records the discovery call and forwards the transcript to Shruti to file here, under the month it happened.

Placeholders

Anything in [BRACKETS] you fill in — the month, and the prospect's name.

File naming — always

Date first, then client, then type: DD-MM-YY_Client Name_Discovery.
