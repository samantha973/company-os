4 · How to fund it properly

You said it yourself — fund it properly. Here's the discipline that means you never over-extend and never need outside capital.

The three funding rules

1 · Cash buffer before every hire. Keep ~3 months of a new person's fully-loaded cost in reserve before their start date. That covers the ramp gap between hiring them and their seats filling.

2 · Hire into demand, never hope. A senior lead only starts when 2–3 accounts are ready to hand over. An idle senior is the most expensive mistake in this model.

3 · Pay yourself, then measure profit. Your A$200k package is a cost, not the leftovers. EBITDA is what's left after — that's the honest number, and it's what funds the next hire.

Model every hire's timing in the financial model before you commit to it. If the buffer isn't there, the hire waits.

6 · The accountability rhythm

The plan only works if it's reviewed. Three loops keep you honest.

WEEKLY · 90 min

Leadership L10

  • Scorecard first — target vs actual
  • Rock status
  • Client health R/A/G
  • Issues solved, not discussed
MONTHLY · 60 min

Founder review

  • P&L + cash buffer check
  • Pipeline & brand-engine health
  • Are we on the quarter's target?
  • Next hire trigger — hit or not?
QUARTERLY · half day

Rock reset

  • Score the quarter's Rocks
  • Client quarterly reports out
  • Set next quarter's 3–5 Rocks
  • Re-forecast the model
Keep yourself accountable

Put the weekly L10 and the quarterly reset in the calendar for the next 12 months today — recurring, non-negotiable, before anything else fills the slots. A plan in a drawer changes nothing. A plan reviewed every Monday changes everything.
