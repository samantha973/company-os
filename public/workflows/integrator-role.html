The Integrator

Your first strategic hire. The person who runs the firm so you can lead it. Get this one right and the bottleneck disappears — you go back to filling rooms and winning the room, and the machine keeps running whether you're in it or not.

Companion to the Build-Out Blueprint · Hire trigger: 3–4 retainers signed

The dynamic

You are the Visionary. This hire is the Integrator. Two seats, one wheel.

Every enduring firm runs on this pairing. The Visionary sees around corners and opens doors. The Integrator turns the vision into a running operation, harmonises the team, and holds the whole thing accountable. Neither is senior to the other — they are two halves of one leadership.

The mandate — what they own

One sentence: the Integrator is accountable for the firm running profitably, on-rhythm, and to standard — without the founder in the engine room.

  • The numbers. Owns the P&L, cash flow, invoicing, and the one-page scorecard. Knows the money cold, every week.
  • Capacity & delivery ops. Manages load points across leads, resourcing, and the workflow systems so nothing drops.
  • The operating rhythm. Runs the weekly L10, holds Rocks on track, drives issues to resolution — not discussion.
  • Hiring & onboarding. Builds the recruitment pipeline, runs process, onboards leads and associates against the model.
  • Client health. Owns the Red/Amber/Green system and renewal radar — surfaces risk before it becomes churn.
  • The bench & systems. Steward of the prompt-library governance, the coverage database, and reporting production.
  • Team & culture. The day-to-day people leader — performance, development, and the in-person cadence.
The line you must hold

The Integrator runs the business; you own the brand and the counsel. The failure mode is you drifting back into operations because it's familiar. Don't. Every hour you spend running the firm is an hour not spent winning the business only you can win.

First 90 days

How you'll know the hire is working — before the probation period is up.

DAYS 1–30

Learn & stabilise

  • Master the model, the bench, the systems
  • Take invoicing & the scorecard off the founder
  • Stand up the weekly L10 properly
  • Map every client's health & renewal date
DAYS 31–60

Own the rhythm

  • Run the L10 unaided; issues getting solved
  • Capacity & load points actively managed
  • Hiring pipeline for Lead #1 underway
  • Reporting production humming
DAYS 61–90

Free the founder

  • Founder out of day-to-day operations
  • Financials clean, forecast reliable
  • First Rock delivered on time
  • Founder's calendar visibly back on sales & brand

Who you're looking for

Green flags

  • Ops/GM/COO background — ideally in a service or agency business
  • Loves systems, rhythm, and finishing things (not the spotlight)
  • Financially fluent — reads a P&L like a story
  • Calm under pressure; holds people to account without drama
  • Genuinely energised sitting behind a visionary, not competing with one
  • Has run a weekly accountability rhythm before (EOS/AOS a plus)

Red flags

  • Wants to be the face / pitch for the founder role
  • Big on ideas, light on execution & follow-through
  • Avoids hard conversations; conflict-averse
  • Needs detailed direction — can't self-start
  • PR-craft ego but no operating discipline
  • Treats finance as "someone else's job"
