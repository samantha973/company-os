Foundation
Sell the outcome, not the output

Never sell "placements." Sell the position: "When someone — or something — searches your category, you're the founder that comes up." That's what a CEO renews on, and it's what makes the earned-media ladder a value story, not a shopping list.

Anchor every proposal to the client's north star, then show which tier reaches it. Price against the business it wins them, not the hours it takes you.

How to price & pitch

Three rules that hold the pricing line

1 · Anchor high, then descend. Present Authority first. It makes Momentum feel like the sensible choice — which is exactly where you want most clients to land.

2 · Quarterly, not monthly. Bill the quarter, aligned to the calendar. Cleaner cash, fewer conversations, and it matches the 90-day plan.

3 · One upgrade lever always visible. Every Foundation and Momentum client should be able to see the next rung. Expansion revenue is the cheapest revenue you'll ever earn.
