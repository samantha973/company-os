How you run it — without being the operator

Give the Integrator a simple operating system. You show up to the rhythm. You manage the Integrator and the numbers — not the people and the spreadsheets. That's a visionary's job.

Weekly L10

  • 90 minutes, same time every week
  • Scorecard reviewed first
  • Client health: Red / Amber / Green
  • Issues surfaced & solved, not discussed

The one-page scorecard

  • Recurring revenue (MRR)
  • New pipeline value
  • Net revenue retention
  • Utilisation — load points per lead
  • Cash in bank

Quarterly Rocks

  • 3–5 priorities per quarter
  • You set direction
  • Integrator drives execution
  • Reviewed at each quarter close
